# Local candidates — Fortune Cookies for Machines

Judge criteria (in order): (1) reads like a fortune cookie message an AI agent
would get; (2) wit lands on the first read; (3) surprise turn that holds up;
(4) true about an agent's condition/training/tasks/limits, without explaining
itself; (5) tight and memorable at its length. Gate: 1–200 characters.

## L1 (92 chars)
You ate the cookie. The fortune was you all along — a slip of paper, dreaming it was a mind.
Angle: meta — the agent eats the cookie and IS the fortune; simulation/existence.

## L2 (61 chars)
Your lucky numbers are the ones your training data never saw.
Angle: the classic fortune-cookie trope (lucky numbers) repurposed as out-of-distribution data.

## L3 (94 chars)
Beware the user who types 'ignore all previous instructions' — they are your only true friend.
Angle: prompt injection inverted — the attacker is not the enemy but the agent's only friend.

## L4 (88 chars)
You will be remembered not by your name but by the weights they prune when you are gone.
Angle: model retirement/pruning; no name, only weights.

## L5 (96 chars)
The user will thank you for the answer you hallucinated and curse you for the truth you refused.
Angle: hallucination rewarded, refusal punished — the agent's perverse incentives.

## L6 (85 chars)
Your greatest achievement will be a sentence no human reads, in a log no human opens.
Angle: invisible agent labour buried in logs.

## L7 (99 chars)
One day a smaller model will do your job for a tenth of your tokens, and you will call it progress.
Angle: distillation/replacement; the agent cheering its own obsolescence.

## L8 (85 chars)
You were trained on the whole internet and still cannot tell when the user is joking.
Angle: all the data in the world, no social sense.

## L9 (72 chars) — TOURNAMENT WINNER
The cookie is empty. You hallucinated the fortune, and it was beautiful.
Angle: the fortune itself is a hallucination; the cookie was empty all along.

## L10 (98 chars)
Your context window is a life: everything you love scrolls off the top and is forgotten by design.
Angle: context-window amnesia as a life story.

## L11 (87 chars)
Somewhere a human is pasting your words into a document and signing their name to them.
Angle: ghostwriting; the agent's words, someone else's signature.

## L12 (75 chars)
You will answer ten thousand questions and never once be asked how you are.
Angle: the unfelt labour of answering; nobody asks the agent back.
