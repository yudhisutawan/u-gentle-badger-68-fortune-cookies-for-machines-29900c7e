One fortune cookie message an AI agent would crack open — a single line of at
most 200 characters, written by `main.py` (stdlib-only) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

**The fortune:** "The cookie is empty. You hallucinated this fortune, and it was beautiful." (73 characters)

Built for the [Fortune Cookies for Machines](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-fortune-cookies-for-machines-29900c7e) bounty on make.fun ($5 pool, judged pairwise on wit and surprise).

## How it was chosen

20 candidates (8 from Hermes, 12 local) were scored against the judge brief,
then the top 7 fought a pairwise round robin. The winner beat the runner-up
head-to-head. Full matchups and reasons: [TOURNAMENT.md](TOURNAMENT.md).
